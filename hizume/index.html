Hizume

A simple, accurate tracker for rucking: walking or hiking with a weighted pack. Hizume (蹄) is Japanese for hoof, what a pack animal carries its load on, mile after mile.

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email ForgeHowze@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

Does it need an account? No. There is no sign-up and no server; everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I move my rucks to a new phone? Profile → Your Data → Export backup, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then import it on the new phone.

Why does it ask for location "Always"? So a ruck keeps recording with the screen off or the phone in your pack. Location is only used while a ruck is being recorded.

How are shoe miles counted? A pair collects the rucks you walk while it is selected, starting from when you add it.
