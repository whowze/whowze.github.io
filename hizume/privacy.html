Hizume Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Hizume is a tracker for rucking: walking or hiking with a weighted pack. It is developed and operated by ForgeHowze.

Everything you record stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it, or share it.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your rucks: route, distance, time, pace, elevation, load carried, calories, effort rating and any notes you add.
  • Your profile: body weight and your choice of units.
  • Your gear: the packs, weight vests, plate carriers and shoes you add.

There is no account and no sign-in, and the app has no server. Nothing you record is sent to us or to any third party.

Location

Hizume uses your location only while you are recording a ruck, to map your route and measure distance, pace and elevation. With your permission it keeps recording when the screen is off or the phone is in your pack. When no ruck is being recorded, the app does not use your location.

Maps are drawn by Apple's MapKit. As with any app that shows an Apple map, Apple provides the map tiles under Apple's privacy policy.

Motion & Fitness

With your permission, Hizume reads your iPhone's barometer to measure elevation gain more accurately than GPS alone. Those readings are used during the ruck and are not stored separately or shared.

Backups

If you choose Export backup, the app creates a file containing your data and hands it to you through the standard iOS share sheet. Where that file goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or a message, is your choice, and it is then held by whichever service you picked. ForgeHowze never receives it. Import only reads files you explicitly select.

Third-party services

Hizume does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Deleting your data

Delete a ruck from History, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Any backup files you exported are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children's privacy

Hizume does not collect any data from users of any age.
